Questions

Straight answers

Is it really zero transaction fees?

Yes. We don't take a percentage or a per-sale charge from your payments. What the customer pays is what you receive.

Why are there no chargebacks?

Customers pay from money they've already added to their Nuclear balance. There's no credit card behind the payment, so there's no card dispute process. If a customer needs their money back, you issue a refund yourself.

How fast do I get paid?

Settlements go out the same day as your sales. Your dashboard shows each payout and its status.

What does the software cost?

Nothing. Catalog, terminals, staff, orders, refunds and payroll are all included with your account.

What do I need to get started?

A business bank account and a few documents. Create your account, upload them, and we'll review your application.
