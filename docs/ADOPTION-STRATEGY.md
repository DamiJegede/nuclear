# Nuclear adoption strategy

## Employer-funded staff wallets

Confirmed by Dami on October 19, 2026 as a path to adoption. This records product direction; it does not indicate an implemented feature or a committed launch date.

### Confirmed model

- Companies use free Nuclear API and platform access, including CSV uploads, to distribute funds to staff.
- Employer pricing is **$0 per employee per month (PEPM)**. Free access is the model, not an introductory trial followed by administration charges.
- Funds move from the **company's own Nuclear wallet to employees' Nuclear wallets**. Companies supply the allowance funds.
- Nuclear does not handle the employer's tax treatment, withholding, or tax reporting for this program. The company determines and handles those responsibilities. Nuclear provides ordinary transaction records.
- This is an employer-funded adoption channel, not an employer administration subscription business. Allowance principal is not Nuclear revenue; monetization comes from separate revenue streams.

### Adoption rationale

One employer can introduce a group of funded users and recurring spending to Nuclear. That spending gives relevant merchants a reason to join, while merchant acceptance makes the employee benefit useful.

The employer receives convenient bulk distribution, reduced gift-card administration, and a visible staff benefit. Employees receive funded purchasing value and access to participating merchants.

### Supporting product direction

- Make bulk funding straightforward, with reusable staff lists, scheduled distributions, and clear transfer results.
- Prioritize useful merchant acceptance near employees' work and homes.
- Direct any additional incentives toward employees. Optional merchant-funded offers can encourage repeat purchases; exact rewards and funding arrangements remain to be decided.

### Guidance for future planning

- Do not reintroduce PEPM charges, paid employer API/CSV access, or a fee-waiver-to-paid-administration model without a new user decision. Earlier assistant suggestions of $1–$3 PEPM and 90-day administration-fee waivers are superseded.
- Do not expand this adoption program into payroll processing or employer tax reporting by assumption.
- Allowance amounts, funding cadence, eligibility rules, rewards, and rollout timing remain open. Prior examples such as $25–$50 monthly are illustrations, not adopted limits or commitments.
- Existing wallet and transfer policies remain applicable. This strategy does not itself change balance limits, cash-out restrictions, or credit decisions.
